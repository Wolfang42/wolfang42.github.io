Jarvis privacy policy

Jarvis is a private assistant that runs on its owner's own computer, for the owner only.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent through Google's sign-in, Jarvis reads the owner's Gmail messages and Google Calendar events, and, when the owner asks and approves, sends email and creates or changes calendar events on the owner's behalf.

Where the data goes

Google data is processed on the owner's computer. The sign-in token is stored only on that computer. Jarvis does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising. When the owner chooses to use a cloud AI model for a request, the text needed to answer that request is sent to that model's provider to produce the answer.

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Jarvis's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
